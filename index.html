Latest News Quick-Check

Sleeper doesn't publish a news feed, so this builds a watch list — your injured/questionable guys, plus the top trending adds — and gives you one-click links to check each one on Google News, X, and RotoWire. This is on-demand, not a background alert — there's no way for a static page with no server to push notifications to you, so you'll want to check this a couple times a week, especially Wed/Thu and right before your lock.

Data Sources

Roster/league/draft data: Sleeper API, live. Player pool & consensus rank: Sleeper's own search_rank. Season production ('25 PPG / GP), draft capital (round/pick), and live depth chart position — all from nflverse-data, an open free dataset, refreshed twice daily. Draft capital and depth chart exist the moment a player is drafted or added to a roster, so they're the main signal available for rookies before any NFL production exists. This app still doesn't have access to paid projection services (FantasyPros, PFF, ESPN, Rotowire) — for a genuinely "best possible" pick on close calls, cross-check those free previews near your deadline.
Data via api.sleeper.app · Player ranks are Sleeper's overall search_rank, used as an ADP proxy — not a paid ranking service.
